About StubHouse

Built by people who hate missing the opening scene

StubHouse started as a weekend project between four friends tired of paper box-office lines. Today it handles seat selection, showtimes, and digital stubs for independent cinemas and small venues.

No app download, no account wall to browse — just pick a seat and go.
